Project Title: Steganography
----------------------------------------------------------------------Project Info-----------------------------------------------------------------
Project Info: 

->Steganography is a practice of concealing a file, message, image,or video within another file, message, image, or video
.Hide secret credentials
.Detect data forgery
.Message Passing etc.
->we would go with digital steganography, In digital method the message is concealed within the lowest bits of images or sound files.
->The current project expects us to implement LSB (Least Significant Bit) Image Steganography on a .bmp file
->This project expects us to Encode a message such a way that the quality of the image doesn't change much.
  So we can do encoding of bits from the message in the LSB of the RGB data
->Decoding is just reverse process of Encoding
